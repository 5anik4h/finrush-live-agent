# Disclaimer

## Not Financial Advice

Finrush is a **personal finance tracker**, not a financial advisor. It:

- ✅ Tracks your money (transactions, budgets, investments)
- ✅ Categorizes automatically via AI
- ❌ Does NOT give investment advice
- ❌ Does NOT predict market movements
- ❌ Does NOT replace a real accountant or advisor

**Big financial decisions? Ask a professional. I'm just a notebook with AI.**

## Accuracy

I try to be accurate, but:

- Exchange rates update every 12 hours (not real-time)
- AI sometimes misunderstands speech (verify unusual entries)
- Rounding errors can occur in complex calculations
- Investment values depend on live market prices (which can be delayed)
- Always double-check important numbers

## Use at Your Own Risk

By using Finrush, you accept:

1. **Server downtime** — Cloud providers have outages. Data stays safe but service may be unavailable.
2. **Data loss** — Back up your data regularly. Export monthly to be safe.
3. **Lost email access** — You can't login without email access (Supabase limitation).
4. **Hacks** — Despite encryption and security, no system is 100% safe.
5. **Service changes** — I might change features, deprecate tools, or shut down.
6. **AI mistakes** — The Gemini AI sometimes misinterprets voice input or extracts receipt data incorrectly.

## What Can Go Wrong

**Voice Input**

- "Spent €50" might be heard as "spent 50" (missing currency)
- Numbers might be misheard
- Accents or background noise might affect recognition
  → Always verify amounts before confirming

**Receipts/Images**

- Complex invoices might be partially extracted
- Poor image quality reduces accuracy
- OCR isn't perfect
  → Verify extracted amounts match your receipt

**Investments**

- Stock prices are delayed (not real-time)
- Your entry price might not match the actual execution price
- Crypto prices move fast; my snapshot might be stale
  → Use this for tracking, not for timing trades

**Currency Conversions**

- Exchange rates cached for 12 hours (not live)
- Historical conversions are frozen at entry time (don't change retroactively)
- Small rounding differences accumulate
  → Use for estimates; verify for important conversions
